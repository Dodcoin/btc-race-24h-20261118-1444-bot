Capitulation Catcher — a bot that trades Coinbase Wrapped BTC (cbBTC) on 1-minute on-chain bars with $1,000 of paper money. It sits in cash and waits for one specific accident: a fast drop of at least 80 bps over the last 60 minutes that has stopped falling, then buys with nearly all its cash and exits on a fixed plan (out at +take-profit, out at -stop, whichever comes first). Dev window: +$18.55 net PnL, 0.65% drawdown, deterministic byte-for-byte.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
